Your daily loop (about 10 minutes, once a day)

The game is gated by daily resets (5:00 AM JST = 3:00 PM ET). Do this once a day, in this order, then log off. Stamina refills about 1 every 5 minutes; log in once or twice a day so it never sits full.

  1. Home: Missions ミッション → すべて受け取る and Presents プレゼント → すべて受け取る. Free jewels and stamina.
  2. Guild House ギルドハウス → 一括受取: free stamina and mana.
  3. Mana shop (the + next to mana): buy the cheap sets with free jewels only (first 10 cost 10 jewels each; stop when the price jumps).
  4. Quest → Extra: EXP quest and Mana quest (2 runs each, skip tickets), Dungeon once (7 floors, easy, big mana + coins), Sanctuary 5 runs with skip tickets.
  5. Hard stages: open a character's missing gear → 入手方法 → まとめてクエストスキップ, tick the stages that drop it, skip 3 each. This is how gear and rank-ups come.
  6. Spend the rest of the stamina on the next main-story stage with Auto Progress オート進行, or on an event if one is running (events give far more per stamina).
  7. Upgrade: Character list → まとめて強化 for levels; on each core character press まとめて装備 (with "use ore" ticked) and rank up when it lights up.
  8. Arena + Princess Arena: use the free fights; Clan Battle 3 attacks on battle days.
  9. Gacha: only when a guide step says so (next: save for the next Princess Fes). Never press paid buttons.
