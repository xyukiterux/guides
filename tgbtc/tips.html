Guild war

Spam filler garrisons

Attackers must beat every garrisoned team one by one, and each fight costs one of their 19 free charges. Late in a war, stack cheap defense teams on strongholds you want to keep. A creator watching a live war: "they're just going to spam some weak teams so the tower can't be taken. Yes, this is the best tactic." ▶ Aslan ~35:00data

Grind defenders down

Defender HP and dead characters don't refresh between fights, so a cheap attack that kills two defenders leaves a half team for your hammer. ▶ The Spectredata

Let the enemy fill a stronghold, then take it

A capture steals 50% of the stored points, so the longer they've held it, the more it's worth. Retake after 45–60 minutes of enemy hold. In the Global war the steal rate is 0, so there you hold instead. datainferred

Hospitals cut revive spend

Winning a fight inside a Hospital tile's area refunds 25% of the HP you lost. Take the ones near your front line early. data

Out-CP-ing bots doesn't work

NPC stronghold bots gain +1% stats for every 1% CP you hold over them, strip all your shields when they die, and Intervene turns off passives. Bring active burst and kill them in one turn. data

Don't withdraw a damaged team you still need

A team that isn't full can't be redeployed once withdrawn ("Team requires 5 characters to deploy"), and a team with a dead member can't be edited. data

Re-check the lineup before the war

The formation screen loads last war's lineup by default after an update added prep time. creator claim

Siege Event (Capture Simulation) scores attempts

+2 points per attack attempt and +10 per enemy team defeated, so just spending attempts scores. Siege bots punish you on death unless you're more than 100k–200k CP above them. data

Stalling no longer wins

In April 2025 a 200-round draw counted as a defeat for the attacker. Fights now cap at 10 rounds and drain 10–50% HP per turn from round 5, so don't build an "unkillable" defense expecting timeouts. creator claimdata

Full field plan: Guild war & Carnival.

Arena

Challenge up

Beating a player rated 100–500 above you pays ×1.1–1.3 points; beating one 300 below pays only ×0.8. Losses to higher-rated players are softened (×0.8–0.9). data

Use your 5 fights every day

Arena gives 5 free challenges a day, refreshed at 05:00 (the rule text), so use them daily rather than counting on them stacking. Real-Time Arena is the mode that recovers 1 attempt per hour. Carnival Arena resets to 10 at 05:00 daily. data

Real-Time Arena windows

11:00–13:00 and 18:00–20:00 server time; daily rank reward at 21:00. data

CP decides who moves first

There is no speed stat. Lower CP gives the enemy the first move. Some kits (Aogiri especially: "the first one wins") swing on this. To test your defense going second, skip your round in a friendly battle, as a creator does ("Should I skip my round and let him start?"). data▶ Aslan▶ Aslan

Auto needs AoE

Have at least one strong AoE unit in every team and auto becomes much more consistent — which matters in the forced-auto Global Pinnacle. ▶ Shi - Gacha

Free test fights

Friendly Battle is real-time against an online friend at no cost: test lineups there before arena. creator claim

Win trading is throttled

The data has cheating counters for repeated matches between the same pair. datainferred

Combat rules that decide fights

Stun stops counters; Disruption doesn't

A stunned unit skips the skills it was about to trigger, including counters and follow-ups. Disruption only strips an active skill's extra effects — passives (Toka [Maid Costume]'s counter, Jason [Natsu Matsuri]'s combos, Nutcracker's Pierce) keep working. data

Attribute cycle

STR > SKL > SPD > STR; PSY and WIT beat each other; DST beats everything and only DST beats DST. Restricting hits deal more damage and add Weak; a full Weak bar stuns for 1 turn. data

The PvP clock

Max 10 rounds. From round 5, every survivor loses 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 50% HP at turn end. Burst teams win long fights. data

Buff strip beats counters

Toka [Maid Costume]'s Patience is a buff, so buff strip (Roma [Anteiku] Purge, Nishiki [Orochi], Touka [Taiikusai]) or passive disable (Ayato [Black Rabbit]'s Limit) stops her. No unit in the data blocks counters directly. data

Team size

Every creator lineup is 4 on the field + 1 back-up. The back-up only uses passives tagged "[Effective also in back-up state]". ▶ Aslan

Cards and upgrades

Merge cards before casting

"Two same and adjacent skill cards of the same stars will compose a skill of more stars, up to 3." Crafting a Lv3 card gives Power Burst, which clears control effects. Fewer, bigger casts also feed fewer Toka [Maid Costume] counters. data

Refinement odds

Shown in game: single ~76%, double ~16%, triple ~8.5%. Potential Lv5→Lv6 synthesis is 1.4%. ▶ Shi - Gacha

Preview 6★ passives for free

Strategic Arena hands out temporary star-up cards: star a unit to 6★ there and read its passives before you invest. creator claim

Compare true CP in Strategic Arena

It normalizes cells, potentials and exclusives, so it's the fair way to compare units; the normal CP list is misleading. creator claim

Bid, don't buy out

On the org auction, bid on cells rather than buying out — much cheaper. creator claim

Potentials

Damage dealers: 4 Smash / 3 Initiate / 2 Undermine or Eliminate (or 6 Initiate / 2 Smash). Tanks: 3 DEF / 6 HP or 6 / 3. creator claim

SSR sphere "trick"

Selecting the UR sphere as target even if you own it may raise SSR sphere drops. The creator himself says "it shouldn't work". unverified

Resources and daily habits

Daily reset is 05:00 server time

Arena, Carnival Arena and phase rewards refresh then. Weekly items reset Monday; Arena week rewards at 21:00, PvP week ends 23:00. data

Friendship points

Run Conquest Battle co-op with an online friend (not the AFK version): both of you get big friendship-point payouts. creator claim

Crimson crystals

Sweep Owl on Hard daily — the best rate per energy. creator claim

Sell old RC cells

Old RC cells sell for gems; one player got about 20k at once. creator claim

Cell Creation tokens carry over

Buy the daily token pack and save tokens for the cell you want. creator claim

UR crystal = Panacea

In the crystal shop, 1 UR crystal = 1 Panacea; two Panaceas start a Carnival unit. creator claim

Wheel of Fortune: one rule

It runs in 21-day cycles data. Take every free spin. The ticket-only buy (~$0.30/day) is optional; its tickets reset when the countdown ends, so start buying right after a reset ▶ Shi 6:53. Never buy the wheel bundles.

Scene batteries

Hoard yellow batteries all week and spend them at the Monday reset. creator claim

Region Breakthrough

3 free sweeps a day; unclaimed rewards are mailed later. data▶ Boss Break the Chains

Pack value

Good: $1 Carnival packs, passes, growth funds, monthly contracts, Random Exchange ticket bundles. Bad: straight gem packs, the daily deal, advancement packs. Details on Pull & spend. creator claim

Share an account safely

Bind a Funcat ID with a changeable username and password instead of handing over a Google or Facebook login. creator claim

Pulls and banners

Selector banners carry over

After 200 recruits you can change your pick, and extra pulls carry into the next round's cumulative rewards, so over-pulling isn't wasted. The novice selector: pull 100 = a chest with a ~10% chance at your pick (otherwise a random SSR), pull 200 = you choose. The in-game rule text says "guaranteed after 100", but the reward config says otherwise — check the pool screen before counting on it. data

Pity

The banner screen shows each banner's pity (the data has 200 and 300 banners). One creator says exclusives are 300 and regular banners 200; regular units later join the standard pool. datacreator claim

Carnival: choose before your first pull

You can only switch the featured unit at 0 draw progress. Tokens carry over between Carnivals. data

Copies are rare

About 4 copies per 300 pulls is "average"; one creator needed 800 pulls for 11 Nutcracker copies. Passing 100 and 200 doesn't guarantee a copy. SSS realistically needs 600–800 pulls plus Panaceas. ▶ Aslan▶ Aslan

New servers: skip Carnival 1–2

Unless the featured unit or the chest list has a unit you lack. Carnival 1's chest is most likely the Christmas list (Kaneki/Hinami [Christmas] or a Lv.6 Potential). creator claimdata

Known bugs and patch log

The Stronghold Annihilation chain-kill bug (seen 2026-04-19).

In a stream, a creator's 1.65M-CP team on a low Annihilation tower stopped losing HP and chained 622 kills alone; often a single surviving character stayed alive and kept killing teams — likely the "one unit takes a whole tower" story. Other players reported it on the 1.5M and 1.6M towers. It looked random, needed many attempts, and every failed try cost revive Diamonds. ▶ Aslan

Patch status: unknown — it's not in the 10-14 fix list or the datamined patch notes. Don't chase it: the game has since banned and rolled back accounts for "maliciously exploiting game bugs", including disqualification from the Global Stronghold War. ▶ Boss Break the Chains
