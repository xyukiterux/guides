Shop packs: is it worth it?

The rule: a plain $4.99 top-up gives 400 Diamonds (80 per $1). The two passes give about 2,365–2,485 per $1, roughly 30× better. A repeating pack (like "$10 every couple of days") is almost never close to that. On your $20 plan: skip it.
