Do NOT do these

  • Don't pull on the novice "Legacy Custom Recruitment". It needs 100 pulls (30,000 Diamonds) for a chest and 200 (60,000) for your pick, and it closes on server day 15. You won't get there.
  • Don't pull on the early banners (Ken Kaneki [Awaken], Juzo [CCG Maverick], Uta [Mask-Maker], Ayato [Rebelwing]). They're launch-era units.
  • Don't pull on Miza Kusakari (live until 10-27): Aogiri, and her guarantee is at pull 200.
  • Never spend Diamonds on: Stamina refills, Arena tickets, chest-timer skips, re-sign-ins (except a missed SSR day: Kurona's day 13, Growth days 7/14/21/28, Novice day 14), Carnival or standard pulls.
  • Don't buy: Diamond top-ups (80 per $1), the $9.99 beginner Rebate Sign-in, or Chain/Streak packs.
  • Save every Event Summon Ticket and Diamond. Goal: 60,000 of banner value (tickets count 300 each) for one 200-pull limited banner later.

Why this team (it works at 0 stars)

Free players sit at 0★ for weeks. In the data, a unit's passive 1 works at 0★, and most faction stat buffs only unlock at 1★ (Sen, Uta [Rescue] and Hideyoshi [Best Friend] are exceptions: theirs work at 0★). These units already do real work at 0★:

  • Sen: +4% basic stats to all allies for each No Organization ally (+16% with four), plus a team debuff-immunity turn.
  • Nutcracker: a 240% bonus attack after every No Organization ally's active skill, healing allies for 20% of the damage.
  • Rize: her stars come free from the mission chain, so she unlocks every passive.
  • Naki (back-up): each turn, allies hit last turn heal 8% of max HP.

Until you have Kurona (day 13) and Naki (day 14), fill with your best No Organization unit or whoever hits hardest.
