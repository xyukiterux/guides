Roadmap: what to run, build, save and pull

Missed the Wish Rebate (it closes as server day 8 begins)? Team 1 is Rize, Sen, Kurona and your best No Organization pull (back-up Naki). Rize takes Nutcracker's place at the top of every resource list below. Your spare $9.99 can stay unspent or go on Endless Trials Pass #2 (~1,882 per $1) once you reach its range.

Server days come from the game's new-server schedule (EU build data; NA can shift by a day or two, so trust your Events menu). Golden rule: no pulls until your bank (Diamonds + Event Summon Tickets × 300) reaches 60,000, then pull once, fully, on a banner that passes the test at the bottom.

The god-banner test: pull only if all 5 are yes

  1. It's an exclusive limited banner (guarantee at pull 200, or 300 for holiday banners).
  2. The unit is No Organization, or buffs every ally.
  3. Its 0★ passive already does real work (you'll be 0★ for a while).
  4. The unit has no shop entry (if a shop sells it later, wait).
  5. Your bank is at 60,000 (90,000 for holiday banners) when it opens.

Banners past 10-27 aren't announced yet in the data. When one appears, tell me its name and I'll run the test for you.
