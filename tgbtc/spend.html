Money rules
  • If you only buy one thing: the Endless Trials Pass #1 ($4.99 ≈ 12,400 banner value, 2,485 per $) data.
  • Spend real money only inside a rebate window, so the purchase also climbs a top-up ladder. Novice Rebate 1 is days 1–7; on this server (day 8 now) the current one is Novice Rebate 2 (days 8–14); next is days 15–21; then 19–25, 26–32 and the big one, days 34–55.
  • Never buy plain Diamonds (80 per $1; 240 with the one-time triple first top-up — about $100 for 24,000 per Shi, still the worst deal ▶ Shi 0:35), the $14.99 Limited-time Sign-in (374 per $), or the Wheel of Fortune bundles (the wheel runs in 21-day cycles data; take free spins; the ticket-only buy is optional — see the creator table).
  • Don't hold money for Black Friday/Thanksgiving — no such event exists anywhere in the game data data.
  • Planning $100 for one event? Make it Christmas: buy the passes and contract Nov 14 – Dec 5 so they stack with the Diamond Rebate; the Christmas sign-in comes ~Dec 12.

F2P pulling rules

  1. Never start a banner you can't finish. Pity counters don't carry between banners data.
  2. Skip any unit with a shop date. A server-day banner's unit enters a shop the day after the banner ends (Juzo: banner 8–14 → shop 15; Uta: 15–28 → 29) data.
  3. What each star buys. 1 copy = the 0★ kit (passive 1). 1★ (one dupe) unlocks passive 2 — usually the faction buff, the best value per copy. 3★ unlocks passive 3, 4★ the Power Burst passive, 6★ the upgraded active data. One creator's rule of thumb is 1 copy or 6★, nothing in between ▶ Zeketta — treat that as opinion: 1★ and 3★ are real power spikes.
  4. Carnival only in blocks of 100. With Diamonds that's 60,000 per block (600 a pull, double a limited pull), so use tickets; on a new server skip Carnival 1–2 unless they hold a unit you lack ▶ Shi. A limited unit is cheaper to star up than a Carnival one ▶ Kitcat 7:46.
  5. Pass every pull through the test: exclusive limited, your faction (or a 0★ buff for any team), useful at 0★, no shop date, and you can reach pity. Full test in the Handbook.
  6. Day-one pulls carry nerf risk. The 10-14 patch cut Nutcracker, Natsu Jason and others ▶ Aslan 10-14.

Where Diamonds go (in this order)

  1. The bank for one banner that passes the test, to its pity (60,000 or 90,000).
  2. Guild-war charges on war day: the first 9 extra attacks cost 690 Diamonds total (50/50/50/80/80/80/100/100/100) — cheaper than reviving data.
  3. Diamond Spring stake (it pays back with profit).
  4. Arena refills only on a fresh server if you're chasing a rank reward ▶ Zeketta — Shi disagrees and says save everything ▶ Shi. Default: don't.

Never: Carnival pulls with Diamonds · base-portal pulls · revives in guild war unless your org calls a push · starting a banner below pity.

Three budgets, dated

$0

  • Bank everything; claim every free track.
  • A $0 account is VIP 0: ~60,000 by day 50 and ~90,000 by day 85 inferred — after Christmas, so your 300-pity target is New Year (Uta [New Year], ~day 91–98).
  • At most one banner before New Year.
  • Shop units on days 43 and 75 are your upgrades — if the listed shop entries are sold.

~$20 a month

  • Month 1 (buy inside Novice Rebate 2, days 8–14 = now to Oct 25, or Novice Rebate 3, days 15–21 = Oct 26 – Nov 1): Chain Level Pass #1 $4.99 + Endless Trials #1 $4.99 (it appears from day 14 at Chain Level 70 data, so in practice inside Rebate 3) + Weekly Pass ×2 $5.98 — or the Halloween sign-in instead of the Weekly Passes if it carries a full unit.
  • Month 2 (Nov 14 – Dec 5): Monthly Contract (if ≤ $4.99) + Endless Trials #2 $9.99.
  • Month 3: Contract + Christmas sign-in $9.99 + New Year Welcome $4.99.
  • Month 4: Contract + Chain Level Pass #2 $9.99.

  1. Endless Trials #2 — $9.99 → 18,800
  2. Monthly Contract ×2 — ~$9.98 check in game → ≈ 14,500 by Dec 26 (first cycle 9,200 if bought Nov 14; the second, stacked Dec 12, pays 800 + 300 × 15 days before the banner ends)

  4. Endless Trials #3 — $14.99 → 25,200
  5. Chain Level Pass #3 — $14.99 → 21,600
  6. Endless Trials #4 — $19.99 → 31,600
