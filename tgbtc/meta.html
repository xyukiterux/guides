Meta teams

The strongest teams the game allows (every released unit at full stars) next to what your account can actually field. Rated from the game's own kit text and stats (EU datamine 2026-09-22). No community tier list covers the 2026 units, so this is the data's verdict. Per-unit ratings are on the full tier list.

Shop dates: every "listed for a shop around day N (unconfirmed)" comes from a shop entry in the data. Whether the shop sells the unit itself, and at what cost, isn't in the data. Same character twice: whether two versions of one character (two Rizes, two Utas) can share a team is unknown. Check the lineup screen before you plan around it.

2. Endgame team per faction

Why you stay No Organization: you own Nutcracker and Sen, and both scale with how many No Organization allies you field. The free bodies (Rize, Kurona, Shu [Gourmet]) and the best shop buffers (Uta [Rescue], Hideyoshi, Nishiki [Orochi]) are No Organization too. Every other faction needs four or five shop or Carnival units before it works. Build the others as guild-war teams 2 and 3, and level them only.

3. Guild war (Stronghold Takeover)

  • HP doesn't come back between fights. Dead characters recover slowly (or with Diamonds/potions), and a team with a dead member can't be edited. Attack teams need burst, shield strip and anti-heal; defense teams need counters, soak and death-saves to drain attackers.
  • Bans: each event phase announces its own ban list. It isn't in the data, so the swaps below are like-for-like guesses. unconfirmed
  • Back-up slot: whether stronghold lineups have one is unconfirmed. The teams below list 5 field units. If a back-up slot exists, put Kiyoko there: her stronghold buff works from back-up.
  • Stronghold specialists: Kiyoko Aura gives all allies +40% basic stats "in Stronghold Takeover" and saves each ally from their first fatal hit. Naki [1.5th Anniversary] (1★) gives +20%, doubled for Aogiri.
  • Only the top 8 organizations in the Weekly Challenge can take part.
