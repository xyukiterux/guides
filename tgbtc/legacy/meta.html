⚠️0. Read this first: why a 4★ Nutcracker team loses to Toka [Maid Costume]

No Organization is not a bad pick. In the newest video, recorded after the 10-14 balance patch, an Aogiri player calls No Org "still number one" and Nutcracker "insanely strong", and says Anteiku is "struggling hardcore" video Aslan 2026-10-19. Before the patch the same creator said Aogiri and No Org were "both way too strong" and Anteiku "completely dead" video Aslan 2026-10-14. That is one creator's read of top-level real-time arena, not an official ranking, but most transcribed videos point the same way: No Org ≈ Aogiri (Black Rabbit + Miza) > Anteiku and CCG. You lose to a Maid Toka team because that account has a complete team built around her, and your team is Nutcracker plus fillers that switch most of her kit off. Here is exactly how:
1. Her counter punishes every active you cast. She holds Patience from battle start and refreshes it at the end of her turn. While she has it, every active skill your units cast during your turn gets a 280% counter on the caster (ignores DEF, triple CRIT Rate), and that caster's next non-active hit is halved. The text has no once-per-turn limit. data SkillDesc_1178_p1, BuffDesc_buff_1178_p1s1_5
Nutcracker's damage is a non-active hit (her 240% Pierce bonus attack), so casting Nutcracker's own active into Patience halves her best hit.
2. You can't one-shot her. At 1★, while she's above 50% HP, no single attack can take more than 45% of her max HP. Below 50% she gets +30% Lifesteal. Each Anteiku ally adds +5% DEF and +8% max HP to her, and every Anteiku ally gets +30% basic stats. data SkillDesc_1178_p2, BuffDesc_buff_1178_p2_6
3. Attribute: SPD beats STR. STR > SKL > SPD > STR; PSY and WIT beat each other; DST beats everything and only DST beats DST. A restricting hit does more damage and adds Weak; a full Weak bar stuns for 1 turn. data LoadingTips2_4, LoadingTips2_2
She is SPD; Nutcracker is STR, so her counters hit Nutcracker harder and Nutcracker's hits on her are weaker (the exact multiplier isn't in the data).
4. Your fillers turn Nutcracker off. Her bonus attack fires only after a No Org ally uses an active; her 1★ +30% basic stats and her 3★ Offensive Mark only go to No Org allies. data SkillDesc_1174_p1, SkillDesc_1174_p2, SkillDesc_1174_p3 Toka [Rabbit] (Anteiku) and a CCG unit give her nothing. The fix is more No Org units: the stronger No Org team in section 2 fields Rize [Seaside], Sen and Roma alongside her. Power (CP) doesn't count any of this. CP only decides who moves first: lower CP gives the enemy the first move. There is no speed stat. data Guidance_Cplow_2, AttributionHeroBase

Rules that decide every PvP fight data Const:PVPMaxRound, Const:PVPOverRoundDamageFactor, EffectInstructionDesc_1_9, EffectInstructionDesc_1_10
  • Turns are team turns. A battle lasts at most 10 rounds; from round 5 every survivor loses 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 50% HP at turn end (rounds 5–10).
  • Stun and full Weak stop triggered skills, including counters and follow-ups. Disruption does not: it only strips an active's extra effects, passives keep working.
  • Merging two identical skill cards makes a higher-level card (up to Lv3); crafting a Lv3 card gives Power Burst, which clears control effects. data TPReviewDesc, TPReviewNP05
  • Teams are 4 on the field + 1 back-up (5 characters) in every creator lineup video Aslan 2026-10-08. The back-up only uses passives tagged "[Effective also in back-up state]".

🏯2. Guild war: rules and your 3 teams

Stronghold Takeover rules data GVG_RuleDesc_1_1_*, Const:GVG*
  • Opens with Carnival. Only the top 8 organizations in that week's Weekly Challenge can enter, so your org's Weekly Challenge score is the ticket (scoring tips below).
  • Every Saturday: preparation from 10:00, battle 12:00–15:00 (server time), rank rewards at 15:00.
  • 3 teams per player. 10 attack charges, +1 every 20 minutes. Lost HP comes back only slowly between fights (a win in a Hospital’s area refunds 25%); dead units recover slowly or are revived with Diamonds or potions.
  • Healing can't lift a defender above the HP it started that fight with data SkillDesc_GVGSys_p1, so burst beats sustain.
  • NPC defenders: Intervene turns off your passives for 1–2 turns, so a passive-only carry (Nutcracker's Pierce is a passive) is weak into them. Fire and Snipe cast Disruption shots; killing one strips all your shields. data SkillDesc_GVGN_p1..p7
  • Capturing another org's stronghold steals 50% of the points stored there. Map play from a real war: take the tier-1s and the nearest tier-2s first, skip the middle tier-3 early (let rivals burn teams on it), use sub-orgs to block enemy routes, and steal late video Aslan 2026-03-14. Timed plan: Guild war field plan.
  • When your first war happens: the data doesn't say. The Carnival rebate event runs server days 19–25, so Carnival (and with it Stronghold Takeover) most likely opens around day 19; the first war would be the Saturday after. That is an inference; check the in-game Carnival timer. data Act:LvReward_04
Getting your org into the top 8: Weekly Challenge data UnionBattle_RuleDesc_1_2_2
  • 5-turn limit. Score = 500 + total damage × 0.004 + best single hit × 0.005 + attribute-restriction damage × 0.0065, then × the difficulty bonus; −20% for every borrowed unit.
  • So bring the attribute that restricts the boss: a blue (SPD) team into a red (STR) boss, green (SKL) into blue, red (STR) into green video Shi 2025-12-07.
  • The creator's core supports for every boss: an SPD Renji in back-up (with his LR cell), Nico [Ladyboy in Love], Sen and Hideyoshi. Nico is in your Unlimited pool and Sen is free. video Shi 2025-12-07

🎯3. How to beat each meta team, turn by turn

Beat Toka [Maid Costume]

F2P account (starter Team 1 with Naki on the field)
  1. Line-up: Nutcracker, Sen, Rize [Binge Eater], Naki on the field, Kurona in back-up (no back-up passive helps here; she only fills the slot). Rize and Naki are SKL and restrict her SPD; your STR fillers don't.
  2. She always starts with Patience (battle start), so each active you cast is one 280% counter. Merge to Lv3 before casting: fewer, bigger casts.
  3. First casts go on her: Rize, then Naki. Restricting hits fill her Weak bar (3–6). Full Weak = stunned for 1 turn = no counters. Naki's active also lowers her skill level, cancelling the +1 she gains every turn. data SkillDesc_1036_v1s1, LoadingTips2_2
  4. Don't cast Nutcracker while she has Patience. The counter would halve Nutcracker's next non-active hit, which is her Pierce. Let Sen, Rize and Naki cast; each No Org cast fires a free Pierce.
  5. She can't lose more than 45% of max HP to one hit while above 50%: plan on 3+ hits. Once she's below 50% she gets +30% Lifesteal. Finish her that turn, or bring Yakumo [Jason] for Lethal (no healing).
  6. Her 3★ Courtesy lands on your highest-initial-ATK unit (usually Nutcracker): −10% damage and Lethal. Sen's first active gives the team debuff immunity for a turn, so cast Sen early. Whether that blocks Courtesy's Lethal depends on timing, which isn't in the data.
  7. Weak you stack on her is wiped at the start of her turn (her talent), so fill the bar in one turn or don't bother. data SkillDesc_1178_bre3_p2/p3
Whale answers
  • Ayato [Black Rabbit] (DST restricts her): his turn-start Strafe is non-active, so it's never countered, and Limit turns her passive (the counter) off once his recorded damage hits 200% of his HP. data SkillDesc_1155_p1
  • Nishiki [Orochi]: strips up to 4 buffs from her whenever she casts (removes Fully Prepared's +40%), and adds +2 Weak per hit.
  • Roma [Anteiku] (SKL): Purge removes all buffs, including Patience. Whether it lands before the counter fires isn't in the data.
  • Miza [Triple Blade]: back-up chip of 10% current HP ignores her 45% cap; Lethal; skill-level lock on her.
  • Disruption does nothing to her counter: it's a passive. data EffectInstructionDesc_1_10

Beat Yakumo [Natsu Matsuri] ("Festival Jason")

F2P account
  1. Count actions. He gains 1 Festival Point every time any unit acts, and at 4 he fires Bash + Rapid Combo (two AoEs, non-active). Since the patch his own combos don't add points. data SkillDesc_1175_p1 video Aslan 10-14
  2. Hit him first with Rize and Naki (SKL restricts his SPD): stun him through Weak or kill him before the 4th action.
  3. Turn 1 against 3+ Aogiri: his 1★ fixes all your extra damage to 1 for a turn, which zeroes Nutcracker's Pierce extra and Underbelly extra. Open with plain hits (Rize 390%, Naki 390%) and save the Nutcracker burst for turn 2. data SkillDesc_1175_p2
  4. Nutcracker's 1★ gives your team −40% extra damage taken, and much of his damage is "extra damage", so keep her alive.
  5. No Org vs Aogiri: go AoE and don't open with Nutcracker video Aslan 09-15. Nutcracker's follow-ups come faster than his, "that's why Jason can't heal".

Beat an Aogiri Black Rabbit team

  1. Win the CP match if you can: "Aogiri is like the first one wins" video Aslan 10-06. A No Org team that moved first beat it in the 10-19 video.
  2. Don't plan on shields or healing. Miza removes shields at her turn start and cuts healing (−60% Restoration, −30% Lifesteal); Limit later sets healing to 0. data SkillDesc_1179_p1, SkillDesc_1155_p1
  3. Kill Jason first (he's their damage and their highest-ATK unit). When Black Rabbit first drops below 50% HP he's immune to all damage for a turn, so hitting him early is wasted.
  4. Black Rabbit is DST, so nothing you own restricts him; burst the supports instead.
  5. With your free roster you won't beat a built one. In guild war, spend charges on weaker strongholds and leave this team alone.

Beat a No Org mirror (a stronger Nutcracker team)

  1. AoE, not single target: "when I play No Org against No Org, I shouldn't use single targets" video Aslan 09-15.
  2. Stop their healing: Yakumo [Jason]'s Lethal (2 turns) blocks the heal on every Pierce. Black Rabbit's Limit does it for whales.
  3. Their Nutcracker's 3★ executes anything below 1% HP and ignores death-immunity, so revive effects won't save you.
  4. Whale counter: Toka [Maid Costume]. A No Org team lives on actives, and she counters every one.

Formidable One-Eyed Owl and Centipede (Conquest)

One-Eyed Owl data SkillDesc_SCru2_*
  1. Bring shields: his turn-start AoE does +500% to units without one.
  2. Kill the adds first: with 3 allies he takes −90% damage. Killing an ally also cancels his Gather Power (+500% next active).

Centipede Kaneki data SkillDesc_SCru3_*
  1. Attributes don't matter (Restriction Ignoring for everyone), and you can't build Weak on him.
  2. Every unit starts Disrupted: rely on passive damage turn 1.
  3. Kill the part first. While it lives he takes −90% damage (Slumber) and his HP can't drop below 40% (Indomitable). When the part dies he Weakens himself: that removes the 40% floor and makes him take +30% damage, but gives him Excitement (+100% ATK stats).
  4. Burst him down while he's Weak. When his Weak ends he casts a 220% AoE that cuts your stats 30%, damage 50% and disables passives for 3 turns.
  5. Shields are stripped at his turn start; Hardshell makes the next hit deal 1, so open with a cheap hit.
