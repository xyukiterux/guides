If you read nothing else
  • Pick one faction and stay in it. F2P: No Organization. Aogiri is co-#1 in the meta but needs Carnival/limited units (Black Rabbit, Miza). Both beat Anteiku and CCG after the 10-14 patch ▶ Aslan 10-19.
  • Bank Diamonds and tickets. A limited unit costs 90,000 (300-pity) to guarantee; a non-limited rate-up such as Miza costs 60,000 (200-pity) data. Never start a banner you can't finish: pity doesn't carry over.
  • Most "limited" launch units come to a shop later. Skip any banner whose unit has a shop date (Juzo day 15, Uta day 29, Hideyoshi and Nishiki day 75) data — the shop entries are in the data; which shop and price are check in game.
  • Join an active org on day 1. Only the top 8 orgs in the Weekly Challenge get into guild war data.
  • Spend real money only inside a rebate window. Novice Rebate 1 is days 1–7; on this server (day 8 now) the current one is Novice Rebate 2 (days 8–14); next is days 15–21. The best is the Diamond Rebate, days 34–55 data. Never buy plain Diamonds (80 per $1).

🎯Pull, skip or save: what's live now

Miza [Triple Blade]

✕ SKIP (No Org) · ends in …

Aogiri support. Strong — creators run her in the Aogiri meta team — but she's banned in the Global Pinnacle and doesn't help a No Org team.

Pull if
  • Aogiri is your main or second team ▶ Aslan 10-13
  • you can reach the pick at 200 pulls (60,000) before Oct 27
Skip if
  • you play No Org
  • you'd stop short of pity (no carry-over)

Evidence: pick at 200 pulls data; creator: "if Aogiri is your second team… use Miza 100%".

Legacy Custom (novice selector)

⏸ SAVE unless you have 60k

300 per pull. 1% shared by your 3 picks; pull 100 is a chest (10% your pick); pull 200 = you choose. Set the picks to Uta [New Year], Rize [Valentine's Day], Rize [Christmas] — none of them has a shop date. This is the novice list; the live calendar Legacy banner (next card) has a different list.

Pull if
  • you have 60,000 in Diamonds and tickets now and play No Org
Skip if
  • you're below 60,000 (30 pulls ≈ 26% for a pick, 100 ≈ 67% incl. the pull-100 chest)
  • you'd rather keep the bank for Christmas

Evidence: data · closes as server day 15 begins.

Legacy Custom (calendar, meta units) · ends ~Oct 29

⏸ ONLY with 60k per copy · ends in …

A separate calendar Legacy Custom banner is live now and lists the current meta units, including Nutcracker, Yakumo [Natsu Matsuri] and Toka [Maid Costume] player report (in game). It isn't in the 2026-09-22 data. Same rules: 300 per pull, pick at 200 pulls = 60,000 per guaranteed copy; check the pick list in game. For a No Org team the best copies are the ones that unlock team-wide 1★ buffs: Rize [Seaside] (+30% No Org) and Roma [Mad Clown] (+18% all, +30% CRIT/CRIT DMG) if listed, then Nutcracker 5★. Don't chase Toka [Maid Costume] as a lone second-team unit: her 45% damage cap and +30% Anteiku buff need 1★, and Anteiku is the weakest faction after the 10-14 patch ▶ Aslan 10-19.

Pull if
  • you have 60,000 per copy you want
  • the copy unlocks a 1★ team buff on your main team
Skip if
  • you'd stop short of 200 pulls
  • the pick is a lone unit for a faction you don't run

Evidence: player report (in game); not in data.

    Carnival 1 (~day 19)

    ✕ SKIP unless it has a unit you lack

    Carnival pulls cost a ticket or 600 Diamonds; each 100 gives a chest — in the Season 1 / 2026 pools 30% featured, 30% Selection Chest, 24% Star-Up, 16% Refinement; Season 2's chest is different (24% featured, no Selection Chest), so read the chest odds in game. Carnival 1's Selection Chest is most likely the Season 1 list: Kaneki [Christmas], Hinami [Christmas] or a Lv.6 Potential — both units are already in the day-19 shop. Hideyoshi and Nishiki are only in a later season's 31-unit chest.

    Pull if
    • the featured unit, or the chest list you see in game, has a unit your team lacks
    • you hold 100 Carnival tickets (never Diamonds)
    Skip if
    • the featured unit isn't your faction (likely Arima [CCG's Reaper])
    • the chest is the Christmas list

    Evidence: datainferred. Carnival Tokens carry over between Carnivals data; whether unused Carnival Summon Tickets carry over is check in game — check before you hold them.
