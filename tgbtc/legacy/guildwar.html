Guild war & Carnival

How to get your organization into the war, what to do on the map hour by hour, how to spend attacks and revives, and how to run Carnival. Built from the game's rule text and constants (EU data 2026-09-22) and from narrated wars on YouTube. The data has no map table (no tier list, point table or road graph), so point values are what creators saw live.

TL;DR
  • Qualify first: only the top 8 orgs in the Weekly Challenge enter; the entry check is Saturday 10:00. Every member posts a score Monday–Friday. data
  • Open on tier-1s next to your base, then the 2 nearest tier-2s. Skip the middle tier-3 early; let a rival burn teams on it. ▶ Aslan
  • Steal late: a capture takes 50% of the points stored there, so retake strongholds the enemy has held for 45–60 minutes, and save 2–3 attacks each for a 14:30–14:50 sweep. data
  • 19 free attacks per player per war (10 + 1 every 20 min). Buying the first 9 extra costs 690 Diamonds total, which is cheaper than reviving. data
  • Defend with tanks and counters, not healers. Healing can't lift a unit above the HP it brought into the fight, and lost HP stays lost. data
  • Carnival 1 most likely opens server day 19 (Fri Oct 30 on the NA relaunch server); hit Chain Level 50 before then, and Arena Gold 5 by Mon Oct 26 05:00 (day 15) — Carnival Arena reads your tier from before 05:00 each Monday. inferred

🧩Before the war: qualify, then give everyone a job

1. Make the top 8 (Weekly Challenge, Mon–Fri)

  • Score = 500 + total damage × 0.004 + best single hit × 0.005 + attribute-restriction damage × 0.0065, times a difficulty bonus. Only your best run counts. 5-turn limit. −20% per borrowed character. data
  • Borrow only if it adds more than 25% damage (1 ÷ 0.8 = 1.25); two borrows need +56%. inferred
  • Restriction damage has the biggest multiplier: bring an attribute that beats the boss (STR > SKL > SPD > STR; DST beats all). Supports the guide creator puts in almost every run: SPD Renji in back-up, Nico, Sen or Uta, Hideyoshi. ▶ Shi - Gacha data
  • The org score is probably the sum of members' bests, so one member at zero costs more than one weak score. inferred
  • Officer routine: post the boss attribute Monday → members post a screenshot by Wednesday → check the top-20 board Thursday → if you're 7th–10th, the 10 lowest scorers retry on a higher difficulty Friday. inferred

3. Preparation (Sat 10:00–12:00)

  1. Lock lineups now. Once a unit dies you can't edit that team. The formation screen loads last war's lineup by default, so check it. data creator claim
  2. Mark 8 targets: the tier-1s next to your Base, the 2 nearest tier-2s, any adjacent special tile (Police Station first), and the middle tier-3 (to watch, not to hit). data
  3. One squad of ~5 members per tier-2, with a leader calling targets in Stronghold chat.
  4. Sister org? Agree on a map split before Saturday. The deciding move in a narrated war was a sub-org walling routes: "this is one suborg … block their way … Building a wall". ▶ Aslan

🗺️The field plan, hour by hour

12:00–12:10 · Spread out
Everyone sends one team down a different road (30 s cooldown per tile). Take every tier-1 next to your Base at once: cheap, an early 300-s point tick, a first-capture reward, and they're path tiles. Take an adjacent Police Station first: it flips its whole area. data ▶ Aslan
↓
12:10–12:40 · Two tier-2s, garrisoned
Squads take the 2 nearest tier-2s and garrison them immediately (3–6 real teams plus filler). A tier-2 is worth 600 vs 75–150 for a tier-1, the best points per charge. Don't open on the tier-3 middle: in the narrated war, the org that took it early was "running out of team because they are taking tier three", and the winner stole it late. ▶ Aslan
↓
12:40–14:20 · Hold, chip, steal
  • Steal on a timer. A retake gives you 50% of what the enemy has stored there, so let it fill: retake after 45–60 minutes of enemy hold, or when the garrison is thin ("Garrisoned Troops x/y" — hit the lowest). data inferred
  • Only take what you can garrison. "If you capture this tower and the enemy captures it back, the enemy gets 600 points." ▶ Aslan
  • Stagger 3 members per target so a fresh team hits every ~100 s (each team waits 300 s). inferred
  • Tier-1 sweep: low-CP members keep retaking cheap tier-1s all war. The winning org "got six tier one towers… Don't underestimate that." ▶ Aslan
  • Rotate defenders: withdraw a garrisoned team below 50% HP and swap in a full one; HP comes back out of combat. Fight inside a Hospital area when you can (25% HP refund per win). data
  • Stop feeding the middle if its garrison is over ~50 teams: that's more charges than 2–3 players have all war. Spend them on tier-2 steals. inferred
↓
14:25 · Reload
Revive the hammers (potions first). Everyone should still hold 2–3 charges plus 1–2 regen. Garrison everything you own: garrisoning costs no charges. inferred
↓
14:30–14:50 · Steal sweep
Use "Check the remaining Stronghold Points" and hit the enemy strongholds with the most stored points (usually the middle tier-3 and their oldest tier-2s). Each steal is a double swing: +50% to you, −50% to them. This is how the winner took the narrated war: "Hudra taking out the point of tier three with tensei… we might have a winner here." data ▶ Aslan
↓
14:40–15:00 · Hold at the bell
Capture your last targets by ~14:40 so protection or a fresh garrison covers the final ticks; don't capture at 14:59 into a counter-push. Strongholds held at 15:00 pay the Finalization share. If scores are close, bank points early: the Global war breaks ties by who reached the score first. data inferred

  • Never sit at 10 charges: regen probably stops at the cap, so spend down to 9 or fewer every 20 minutes. inferred
  • Budget: F2P buys the first 3 charges (150 Diamonds); mid spenders the first 9 (690); whales all 19. inferred
  • Buy a charge before you revive. After the 5th revive each character costs 150+, so a ≤100-Diamond charge is cheaper — unless the revive is for a top-CP team that wins fights alone. inferred
  • Waiting is free: dead units recover slowly and HP returns ~1%/min out of combat (~100 min from empty). Let wall and filler teams wait. data inferred
  • Potions before Diamonds for hammer teams. Diamond and potion revives also earn Revival Points; the Revival Point Shop's best item is the Panacean Star-Up Crystal. data
  • Revive cap: after 300 revives it's "Quick revive unavailable… please wait". Late in a war, orgs ran out: "the highest CP people are going for the towers now". data ▶ Aslan
  • Spend schedule: 12:00–12:40 the starting 10 + 2 regen (captures) · 12:40–14:20 ~5 regen (steals) · 14:20–15:00 hold 2–3 + regen (final sweep).

🛡️Defending: make them pay for every stronghold

  • Tanks, death-saves and counters, not healers. The heal cap stops in-battle healing above the HP a unit brought in, and lost HP stays lost, so effective HP and punishing the attacker's actives win the attrition war. data Best slots: Kiyoko Aura (+40% basic stats in Stronghold Takeover, first fatal hit saved at 1 HP) data, Toka [Maid Costume]'s counters, Uta [The Past] pursuit, Urie's fatal pursuit (see the Tier list).
  • Stack depth, not just quality. Real teams first, then filler. "They're just going to spam some weak teams so the tower can't be taken. Yes, this is the best tactic." Every team the attacker must beat costs a charge and HP that doesn't come back. ▶ Aslan ~35:00 data
  • Worn defenders stay worn. Dead defenders don't come back between fights, so cheap attacks can grind a garrison down for your hammer. ▶ The Spectre
  • Put your strongest defender on a known chokepoint: the Defense honour rank counts attackers repelled and pays 10–20 Exclusive Treasure Box keys to the top. Barracks areas make your dying units hit back for 1% max HP. data

🤖NPC (bot) strongholds

Bot defenders are CCG Battlers. Their kit data:

  • Attribute Boost: +1% to their basic stats for every 1% CP you have above them. Out-CP-ing them is cancelled out.
  • Intervene: turns off your passives for 1–2 turns. Fire / Snipe cast Disruption shots. Guardian casts Provoke.
  • Revenge / Covering / Emergency Firing: 210% hits when a bot dies, is Weakened, or first drops below 50% HP.
  • Death Cry: on death strips all your shields and deals 5% of its max HP. Emergency Shield: 20% of lost HP as shield each turn; shielded bots +20% damage dealt, −20% taken.

So bring: active-skill burst (not passive engines — Nutcracker's Pierce is a passive and Intervene shuts it off); shield strip (Miza, Roma [Mad Clown], Naki [1.5th Anniversary]); Naki [1.5th Anniversary] for Disruption immunity (his passive 2, so he needs 1★) data; and kill the line in as few turns as possible, because every death and every Weak proc fires 210%. Don't sink Diamonds into bot towers: after a Carnival change players found them "much harder" than player teams. ▶ description inferred

🌐Global Stronghold War and the Global Pinnacle Championship

Global Stronghold War (Sundays)

  • Cross-server. Qualifying needs a top-512 org by total member CP, then Qualifiers → Promotion → Knockout → Championship. Prep 13:00, battle 14:00–15:00. 3 teams, 10 charges. data
  • No stealing here (steal rate 0); tiles pay +10 / +30 / +50 per battle, so holding beats stealing. Cooldowns are 10 s, not 300 s. data
  • Each phase announces its own character bans (not in the data). Total member CP is the entry ticket, so recruit high-CP members. check in game
  • Season 1 ran 2026-08-19 → 09-28 with Chain Level ≥21. Whether a new server can enter Season 2 is unknown. data check in game

Global Pinnacle Championship (Oct 23 – Nov 22)

  • Shown for an EU server: event Oct 23 – Nov 22, preparation Oct 20–30; logging in during prep registers you. Whether a new NA server is eligible isn't known inferred.
  • Cross-server, all auto-battle, 5 teams each, best of five. Lineups and CP are visible during a 10-minute lineup lock, so order your 5 teams to answer theirs. ▶ Aslan
  • Banned: Ayato [Black Rabbit] and Miza [Triple Blade]. ▶ Aslan
  • Auto favours teams with at least one strong AoE unit. ▶ Shi - Gacha
