The whole plan in five lines
  • Days 1–14: free units, Chain Campaign, join a top org, skip every banner, bank everything.
  • Days 15–42: first guild wars; Weekly Challenge every week; skip Carnival 1–2 unless they hold a unit you lack; buy Uta [Rescue] on day 43 if the shop sells him.
  • Days 43–90: bank 90,000 — VIP 5 has it by ~day 62 (Christmas), VIP 0 by ~day 85 (New Year, Uta [New Year]); buy Hideyoshi on day 75 if sold.
  • Day 75+: full No Org team at 0★ (every buff that matters works at 0★). Now stars and gear.
  • Never: pull a banner whose unit has a shop date, start a banner you can't finish, or buy Diamonds directly.

Early game · days 1–14

Goals (check them off)

  • Join the most active org you can find on day 1. Org income and guild-war entry depend on it ▶ Zeketta.
  • Rush the Chain Campaign: it pays the most early Diamonds ▶ Shi and opens everything (Chain Level 17 banners, 50 Carnival rebate, 70 Endless Trials Pass).
  • Spend every point of half-cost Stamina (days 1–28, double drops) data.
  • Pull Unlimited Recruitment with its free tickets for Sen [Elegant Deception] (days 1–14 only).
  • Claim the free units: Enji (sign-in day 1), Yakumo [Jason] (day 5), Kureo (day 9), Kurona (day 13), Naki via the day-14 SSR Selection Card, Rize [Binge Eater] from the Full-Stars Rize mission chain.
  • Two clocks: Unlimited Recruitment runs your first 14 days after you register data. The Beginner sign-in (server days 1–14) and the Celebration sign-in (server days 1–28) are fixed server-day windows data — if you start after day 1 you get fewer sign-ins, so late starters miss Kureo, Kurona and the day-28 card.
  • Arena every day; reach Gold 5 by Mon Oct 26 05:00 (day 15) at the latest — Carnival Arena takes your Arena tier from before 05:00 each Monday, so Gold 5 on days 16–18 is too late for Carnival 1 data.
  • Hit the hero level cap: 135 (day 8) → 165 (day 14).

Pull / skip

  • Skip the day 1–7 and day 8–14 featured banners (both units come to shops).
  • Skip the novice Legacy selector unless you have 60,000 to reach its pick.
  • The live calendar Legacy Custom banner (ends ~Oct 29 player report (in game)) lists Nutcracker, Natsu Jason and Maid Toka: 60,000 per guaranteed pick. No Org: spend it on 1★ team-buff dupes (Rize [Seaside], Roma) before Nutcracker 5★; skip a lone Maid Toka.
  • Base portal: tickets only, never Diamonds ▶ Zeketta.

Save

Everything. Expected bank on day 14: roughly 15,000–25,000 + tickets inferred.

Mid game · days 15–60

Goals

  • Three full teams at the level cap by day 19 — guild war takes 3 teams per player data. Level the bodies; star only the main team.
  • Chain Level 50 by day 19 (Carnival Support Project rebate needs it) and 130 by day 40 (Abyssal Battlefield).
  • Every org member posts a Weekly Challenge run Monday–Friday — it decides the top 8 for Saturday's war data.
  • Carnival: put Carnival Tokens into one Paragon team; spend Carnival Arena tickets daily by 21:00 to be safe: rewards settle at 22:00 data and challenges close before that (the game text says 1 hour before, the config 21:30) data.
  • Day 43: buy Uta [Rescue] (and Kiyoko Aura if your org fights a lot — +40% stats in Stronghold Takeover) data.
  • Level caps: 180 (day 20), 200 (day 30), 215 (day 42).

Pull / skip

  • Carnival 1–2: skip unless the featured unit or the in-game chest list has a unit you lack ▶ Shi. If you pull, only in blocks of 100 tickets — Diamonds cost 600 a pull.
  • 2nd Anniversary: claim every free sign-in; pull only a unit that passes the test, or the anniversary Legacy selector if it lists a No Org limited you lack.
  • Ayato [Rebelwing] (days 26–42): skip.

Save

60,000 by day 58 at minimum. 90,000 by day 62 if you're VIP 5 and want Christmas; at VIP 0, 90,000 by ~day 85–90 for New Year. Carnival tickets in multiples of 100. Shop currency for days 43 and 75.

End game · day 60+

Goals

  • Day 75: buy Hideyoshi, then Nishiki [Orochi] (second carry and ban insurance) — if the shop sells them. Your No Org team is then complete at 0★.
  • One No Org holiday unit to pity: Christmas at VIP 5, New Year (Uta [New Year]) at VIP 0.
  • Force Talents every week (linear: 50,000 papers ≈ +2,500 CP, permanent) ▶ Shi. Order: middle branch, then top, main org first ▶ Zeketta.
  • Stars on the carry: 5★ is the biggest jump. Use Panacean Star-Up Crystals here (Revival Point shop sells them) data.
  • Start the second faction (Aogiri: Black Rabbit, listed for the day-75 shop, price unconfirmed) for guild-war team 2.
  • Level caps: 235 (day 60), 250 (day 77), 265 (day 98), 280 (day 119).

How a free player actually gets to the top

You won't out-pull a whale on a new limited. You win on the things that don't cost Diamonds:

  1. A complete faction team beats a higher-CP mixed team. CP only decides who moves first data; the faction buffs (+30%, +15%, +4% per ally) decide the fight.
  2. Free and shop units fill 4 of 5 slots by day 75 — if the listed shop entries are sold. That's ~2.5 months. Most players on a fresh server waste Diamonds on launch banners whose units hit the shop a week later.
  3. Your bank buys one unit at full pity (Christmas at VIP 5, New Year at VIP 0) instead of three half-finished banners.
  4. Guild war is an org sport. Charges, timing and garrisons win wars more than any single team — see Guild war.

Honest ceiling: around day 100 a disciplined F2P No Org account has every 0★ buff that matters plus one holiday unit. Whales will still have stars and LR cells; you beat them with counters (turn plans) and by not wasting attacks.
