Tokyo Ghoul: Break the Chains · Unlimited Recruitment + early game

Who to keep, who to skip

Deadline: Unlimited Recruitment is open for your first 14 days after you register (registration-day clock, not server days). For an account made on server day 1 of the NA relaunch server that is through Sun Oct 25; it closes as your day 15 begins (Mon Oct 26 05:00 server time).

This page covers the Unlimited Recruitment pool only. All 121 units, rated at 0★ and at full stars: Full tier list. The best teams per faction and mode: Meta teams.

Unlimited Recruitment lets you re-roll until you confirm one result, and confirming is final. Every 10-pull has exactly 1 SSR from these 42 units (about 2.4% each). Ranked from the game data on three things: what the unit does at 0★ (where a free player stays), whether it fits a No Organization team, and whether a shop sells it later.

SConfirm the moment you see it

Sen Takatsuki [Elegant Deception]
NoOrg · SPD · released 2026-01-24 · stat mult 1.57

Confirm this one. No Organization. At 0★: +4% basic stats to ALL allies per No Organization ally (+16% with four), and team debuff immunity after her next active. No shop entry, so this is your chance.

CCG carry engine (Blade Boost stacks from CCG actives). No shop entry. His team buff needs 1★.

Anteiku · STR · released 2026-02-21 · stat mult 1.56

Anteiku. Works from back-up and Assistance; buffs all allies with stacks at battle start (the exact effect isn't spelled out in the data). No shop entry. Fine fallback if Sen won't appear.

You pulled Nishiki Nishio [Anteiku]: an A-tier fallback, not a bad pull. Keep re-rolling for Sen while your re-roll window lasts. If Sen never shows, Nishiki is the next-best keep for a back-up slot.

When you pull with your free Summon Tickets

  • Use: your free Summon Tickets only reach the 21 launch-era SSRs (no 2026 units). Keep No Organization ones first; duplicates of units you own become stars. 2026 units (stat multipliers around 1.5–1.7, against about 0.95 for launch units) come from limited banners, sign-ins and shops.
  • Level only: launch-era SSRs, as fillers for teams 2 and 3 in guild war.
  • Never put stars or rare materials into fillers.
