Anteiku · Make Coffee

☕ The coffee recipe book

All 24 formulas, checked against the in-game Recommended Formula screen. Each one is 6 ingredients from a pool of 6. Coffee is a slow side system: it pays gold, Summon Tickets and Diamonds, and it feeds your Organization's weekly Supply orders.

24recipes (12 iced, 12 hot)
5rarities each, N → UR (luck)
120illustrations to collect
11 + 2,000Summon Tickets + Diamonds from illustration milestones
TL;DR
  • The rule: two-ingredient drinks are 5 : 1 (base : main, or the reverse). Three-ingredient drinks are 1 base + 4 + 1 or 1 + 1 + 4.
  • The formula only picks the drink. Its rarity (N/R/SR/SSR/UR) is pure luck, so repeat each recipe until all 5 rarities are in the Illustration book.
  • Hand your spare coffees in to Organization → Supply instead of selling them. You get the same gold plus order rewards. Keep SSR/UR cups for the “SSR rarity or above” orders.
  • Ingredients are scarce. The steady sources are the Anteiku Checkout Ingredient Box, the one-time story-stage drops, and events.

How Coffee Making works

  1. Open Anteiku from the bottom bar (between Rank and Achievements), then tap Make Coffee.
  2. Pick ingredients: one Base (Iced/Hot Coffee), one Main (Milk/Chocolate), and one Minor (Dried Nuts/Spice) or None.
  3. Set the ratio: a cup needs 6–10 ingredients. Use the counts on the cards below (always 6).
  4. Brew Once or Multiple Times (up to 99 crafts). Every cup rolls a random rarity from N to UR.
  5. Collect: each new drink + rarity unlocks an illustration and counts toward the milestone rewards. Extra cups go to Organization Supply.

Sources: UI text Coffemaking_UI_2/3/4, GuideDialogue_Cafe_2/7/13/19; ingredient slots and the 6/10 limits are Const rows CoffeMaterial, MinimumCoffeMaterialRequired and MaximumCoffeMaterialRequired. The 99 cap is Coffemaking_Tips_7. Rarity being random comes from StillAliveGG, abb-Mo7R5zc @1:18.

Rarity, selling and Organization Supply

What a cup is worth

Every drink comes in 5 rarities, and each rarity is its own item. Sell price and Supply value are the same:

N10k gold
R20k
SR30k
SSR50k
UR150k

From G_item sell_price / order_price. Item ids are N 22008–22031, R 22044–22067, SR 22080–22103, SSR 22116–22139 and UR 22152–22175.

Organization Supply (weekly)

  • Your Organization gets a set number of orders each week, and they refresh weekly. Anyone can turn coffee in. When an order fills, a new one appears until the week's allowance is used up.
  • Each hand-in pays gold equal to the cup's value. Members also get a reward for each completed order, and the bonus can be claimed once per week (changing Organization does not reset it).
  • Order types are Any Coffee, Any Iced, Any Hot, Any Mocha, Any Latte, Any Frappuccino, and R / SR / SSR rarity or above. Fill the “any rarity” orders with N cups and save SSR/UR cups for the SSR order.

Sources: UnionOrder_RuleDesc_1_1_1…5, UnionOrder_Name/Desc_1…11, StillAliveGG @4:41–6:26. Order rewards seen in the video were gold, Exclusive Treasure Box Keys and Combat Manuals.

Quests that want coffee

  • Battle Pass weekly: “Make Coffee 10 times” / “Make coffee 5x” (BattlePass_1/2_weekly13).
  • Event pass weekly: “Submit 50 Coffees for Organization” (ActBattlePass_1_weekly8).
  • Quests: “Make %1% servings of Coffee” / “Unlock %1% type(s) of Coffee” (TaskDesc_130/131).
  • Titles: Novice Barista (10 coffee types), Intermediate Barista (all types), Advanced Barista (all types and all rarities) (PlayerTitleCoffee_1–3).

Coffee Ingredient Box

Opens into 1 random ingredient, with an equal ~16.6% chance for each of the six (CoffeMaterialPack). Main sources:

  • Anteiku → Checkout builds boxes up passively. One account showed max storage 20 and 39.3 min per box at max efficiency (@6:54). Claim before it caps.
  • Story stage x-12 clear rewards in chapters 1–10 (ClearReward_M01…M10F12_1), plus 20-box stage chests (Cam105_Box03/07) and event rewards (e.g. ActSearchFlower01_1, 3 boxes).

Every other source in the data

  • Story stages: in chapters 1–10, stages x-4…x-9 each give one specific ingredient once (Iced, Hot, Milk, Chocolate, Nuts, Spice in that order). Source: ClearReward_M01F04_1…M10F09_1, 60 drops.
  • Event stages (Pursuit-style limited events, e.g. Perilous Time) have one loot roll that drops a random ingredient: Acta*_7_1 (100 tables) and ActStage*_G*_4 (15 tables).
  • Challengeable event bosses add a random ingredient to every difficulty tier (ActBoss2/5/11/13/17/20_cofe).
  • Continuous-login rewards roll 5× of one ingredient (5% per ingredient, ContinuousReward01_01).
  • New accounts start with 2 Iced Coffee, 2 Milk and 2 Dried Nuts (Reward_init). That's one Iced Almond- or Oat-Flavored Latte short of a full set.

Creator advice: ingredients come slowly, so treat coffee as a passive side task and look for events that drop them (@6:33–7:48).

The coffee-cup puzzle (Maid Cafe event)

Rules

  • Part of the limited School Festival event (last run 2026-09-29 → 10-13, Chain level 18+). The same minigame ran as Jingle Bell (Dec 2025) and Candy Link (Mar 2026), so expect it again.
  • Each round starts with a number of Order Counts. Every order drops a random-coloured cup into a 3×3 grid. You start with 39 orders (day 1–2), 49 (day 3–4) and 59 (day 5+).
  • Slide to connect same-coloured cups in a line to clear them. Longer chains score more points and refund orders.
  • The round ends when no cups are left to order and no match remains on the grid.
  • There are 10 cup colours. Colours 1–5 are 3× as common as colours 6–10, and the last three only appear later in a round (BellWeight, BellUnlock).

Sources: Gakuensai_RuleDesc_1_4_1…6, GuideDialogue_26Gakuensai_1–3, and the ActSchoolFestivalBell_2026 config.

Tools

  • Broom: clears any row or column and counts as a ×3 (40 pts, no orders back). Unlocks at a Day-1 best of 10 pts.
  • Position Swap: swaps two adjacent cups. Unlocks at a Day-1 best of 400 pts.
  • Order Bell: recolours any cup to one of the other 9 colours. Unlocks on Day 3 with a cumulative best of 600 pts.
  • Uses per round: 2 at first, rising to 4 as the event goes on. Bells are 0 on days 1–2.

Tips

  • Go long, not fast. A ×9 pays 27.8 pts per cup and a ×2 pays 10. From ×4 up, chains also refund orders, so the round lasts longer.
  • Hold off on clearing a ×2/×3 while the grid still has room. Let a colour build into a snake first.
  • Save the Broom for a jammed grid with no move left. It never refunds orders.
  • Use Swap and Bell to join two chains of the same colour into one ×6+ chain. Recolour a rare-colour cup (6–10) to match the colour the grid is full of.
  • Your best single round sets the point milestones: 300 → 2 Gemstone Bunny … 800 → 1,000 Diamonds, with Summon and Event Summon Tickets in between. There's also a daily reward for playing once (SchoolBellPointReward01–10, SchoolBellDailyReward).

These tips are worked out from the scoring tables above, not from a creator guide.

What we couldn't confirm

  • Rarity odds: the drop chance for each rarity isn't in the datamine. Creators only say it's random.
  • Off-recipe ratios: it's not confirmed what happens with other mixes (e.g. 2:4, or 7–10 ingredients), or whether bigger batches change the result. Stick to the formulas above.
  • Milestone thresholds for the 46 illustration rewards (see above).
  • Anteiku unlock: the level or stage that opens Anteiku isn't in the datamine. Something also unlocks after making coffee a set number of times (Tips_UnlockSystem_GetCoffee), but the number isn't given.
  • Affection, bonds and buffs: nothing in the data links coffee to character affection or battle buffs. The “makecoffee” exploration events (all_makecoffee_01–03) are chest choices in Exploration and have nothing to do with brewing.
  • Witch Spice (ItemName_22007, “might impart some amazing flavors and magic”) has text but no item row, and it isn't in the ingredient list. It looks like cut or future content.
  • Cubed Sugar Coffee isn't a recipe. It's a pre-registration avatar frame (HeadFrame_HF_Reservation_1).
