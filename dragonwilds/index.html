COVERAGE

What is illustrated, and what is described

1,456 of 1,575 records (92%) carry real game art, pulled from the published databases and the official wiki and normalised to one size. 1,123 carry the in-game description, 529 carry journal lore, and 103 of 169 creatures are illustrated. The rest show a category glyph — see the note below.

The 119 records without art were checked against the shipped game files: 65 do not exist in the game at all (datamined names that never shipped), 13 are dataset groupings such as “Amulets”, 5 are interaction prompts such as “Examine”, and 1 is a real item the game itself has not drawn an icon for. There is nothing left to find, and no substitute art has been invented.
