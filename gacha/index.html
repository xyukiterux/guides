Gacha Picks

A shortlist to choose from. It starts with games that feel like Tokyo Ghoul: Break the Chains and Princess Connect (anime characters, auto or semi-auto battles, dailies, guild bosses). After that come the big polished ones, the ones you can play with friends, and the ones that are kindest to free players.

Checked 22 Nov 2026 against the sources at the bottom. A green checked tag means a 2025–26 source confirms the game is still running. A gold from memory tag means I didn't find a fresh source for it this time. The dot ratings are my own judgement, not a site's score.

If you only read one thing

Closest to Princess ConnectBlue Archive

Cute chibi auto-battles, a huge cast, lots of story, daily routine and raid bosses. Free players do well. Runs on phone and Steam.

Closest to Tokyo GhoulBleach: Brave Souls

Anime license, dark shonen cast, and quests friends can actually play together. Over ten years old and still getting updates.

Runner-up, anime licenseSeven Deadly Sins: Grand Cross

Turn-based card battles with anime-quality cutscenes, guild bosses and lots of anime collabs. 70M+ downloads, 7th anniversary in 2026.

Good to know
  • Princess Connect! Re:Dive outside Japan shut down on 30 April 2023. The Japanese version is still running, so the JP account is the only way to play it.
  • Almost every gacha is a solo game. "With friends" usually means a guild boss, borrowing a friend's character, or comparing pulls. Only Wuthering Waves, Genshin, Bleach: Brave Souls and Tower of Fantasy let you play the same fight together live.
  • Free-to-play tip for any of them: don't pull on every banner. Save for characters you actually want, because pity carries over in most games.
